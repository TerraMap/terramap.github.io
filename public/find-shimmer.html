How to find Shimmer in Terraria

Every world made in Terraria v1.4.4 or later has a single pool of Shimmer, inside a mini-biome called the Aether. Here's where it generates, and how to find it on your own world's map.

Where the Aether generates

  • On the Jungle side of the world, which is the opposite side from the Dungeon.
  • Near the edge of the world, usually within its outer fifth.
  • Deep underground, in the Cavern layer, around halfway down the world.

It's a large oval cave of stone with gem trees inside and the Shimmer pool at the bottom. There's only one per world, so before you defeat the Moon Lord it's the only Shimmer you can get.

Worlds created before Terraria v1.4.4 don't have an Aether, and one won't be added to an existing world. You'll need to make a new world to get one.

Find it with TerraMap

  1. Open TerraMap and open your world with Open World File, or drag your .wld file onto the page. Not sure where it is? See where Terraria world files are saved.
  2. Press Z (Zoom to Fit) to see the whole world.
  3. Press D (Go To Dungeon) to see which side the Dungeon is on. The Aether is on the other side, with the Jungle.
  4. Look near that edge of the map, about halfway down, for a grey oval with a light purple pool. TerraMap draws Shimmer in light purple, and no other liquid is that color, so it stands out even when zoomed out.
  5. Zoom in with the mouse wheel and click the pool. The Info pane (I) shows Liquid: Shimmer and the pool's location.

Tip: TerraMap shows locations the way a Compass and Depth Meter (or a GPS) show them in game, like "2300 West, 374 ft Cavern", so you can dig straight to the pool.

Want to avoid spoilers? Open your Terraria folder with Folder and choose a character, and TerraMap only shows what that character has explored.

When you get there

Be careful jumping in: being submerged in Shimmer makes you fall through blocks. Items you drop into it transform into other items, or break back down into the materials they were crafted from. The Terraria Wiki's Shimmer page lists every transformation.
