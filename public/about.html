Explore your whole Terraria world on one map

TerraMap is an interactive world map viewer that runs in your browser. Open a world file to pan, zoom and find ores, chests and the items in them, Shimmer, Enchanted Swords, the Dungeon, NPCs and more.

Open TerraMap Get the desktop app

Features

Safe and private

Completely read-only, so your world can't be changed or corrupted. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

No spoilers

Open your Terraria folder and pick a character to see only the parts of the world that character has explored.

Find anything

Search for any block, wall or item. Jump between matches with Find Next and Previous, or Highlight All to see every match at once.

Ready-made sets

One click finds chests, locked chests, Life Crystals and Life Fruit, Enchanted Swords, Spider Caves, item-spawning statues and Corruption, Crimson and Hallow blocks.

Chest contents

Hover a chest to preview what's inside, find chests holding a specific item, and open any item on the Terraria Wiki.

In-game coordinates

Locations are shown the way the Compass and Depth Meter show them, like "2300 West, 374 ft Cavern", so you can walk straight there.

World details

See world properties, the spread of Corruption, Crimson and Hallow, and jump to the spawn point, the Dungeon or any NPC.

Wires and images

Show the world's wiring, and save a full-size PNG image of the whole map.

Works everywhere

Any modern browser on Windows, macOS and Linux, with keyboard shortcuts, light and dark themes, and English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.
