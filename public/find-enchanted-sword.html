How to find an Enchanted Sword Shrine in Terraria

The Enchanted Sword is one of the best early swords in the game, and the only place to get one is an Enchanted Sword Shrine. They're rare and well hidden, but TerraMap can find every shrine in your world in a few clicks.

What you're looking for

An Enchanted Sword Shrine is a small underground cave with a sword stuck in stone. Mine the sword and it drops an Enchanted Sword, or, with a 1 in 50 chance, a Terragrim instead (in worlds created in Terraria v1.4.0.1 or later).

  • Not every world has one, and some worlds have more than one.
  • They can be anywhere from just below the surface to deep in the Cavern layer.
  • Some have a narrow, one-block-wide shaft leading down into them, but most are sealed off and easy to walk right past.

Find it with TerraMap

  1. Open TerraMap and open your world with Open World File, or drag your .wld file onto the page. Not sure where it is? See where Terraria world files are saved.
  2. Choose Sets → Enchanted Items. This searches for swords in stone, and for chests holding an Enchanted Sword or an Enchanted Boomerang.
  3. Press H (Highlight All). Everything else on the map dims so the matches stand out.
  4. Press N (Find Next) to jump to each match in turn, and P to go back. TerraMap centers the map on it and marks it with a pink target. Zoom in with the mouse wheel or E for a closer look.
  5. Press I to open the Info pane. A shrine's sword shows as Variety: Enchanted Sword, with its location, like "2300 West, 374 ft Cavern".

Tip: TerraMap shows locations the way a Compass and Depth Meter (or a GPS) show them in game, so you can dig straight down to the shrine.

No matches? Your world doesn't have a shrine. If you opened a folder and chose a character, TerraMap only searches what that character has explored, so open the world file on its own to search everywhere.

Other things worth finding

The Sets menu has more one-click searches: Life Crystals and Life Fruit, locked chests, Spider Caves, item-spawning statues, and Corruption, Crimson and Hallow blocks. You can also search for any block, wall or item with Blocks (B), including items inside chests.
